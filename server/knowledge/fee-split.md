# Fees and who gets what

## What a customer pays
The customer pays the worker's rate plus a 10% service fee. GST is added where it applies. The quote a customer sees before booking lists every line, and the total does not change between the quote and the booking.

## What the worker receives
The worker receives 100% of their own rate. Nothing is deducted from the worker for the society, the welfare pool or the platform. Those come out of the 10% service fee the customer pays on top.

## How the 10% service fee is split
- 5% goes to the worker's cooperative society.
- 3% goes to the district welfare pool.
- 1% goes to the workmanship guarantee reserve.
- 1% goes to the platform.
A worker who is not in a society has the society share go to their district federation, or to the state federation where there is no district one.

## GST
GST is charged where it applies to the job and to the service fee. [[TO BE CONFIRMED: the GST rate on the service fee, to be confirmed with a chartered accountant before it is stated to customers.]]

## Invoices
A tax invoice is available for each completed booking and shows the service fee as its own line. Institutions with a contract get one consolidated monthly invoice.
