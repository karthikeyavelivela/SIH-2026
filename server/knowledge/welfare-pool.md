# The welfare pool

## What it is
Each district has a welfare pool, filled from 3% of the service fee on every booking. It pays members when work in their district or society drops well below normal, through no fault of their own.

## What sets it off
One worker having a slow week does not set it off. The check looks at demand across a whole district, and across each society that has enough members. Every week it compares completed bookings per active member with the normal level, which is the median of the previous 12 weeks. If this week is below 0.6 of normal, the pool pays.

## Who counts as active
A member is active if they were available or working on at least 8 of the last 28 days.

## How much is paid
A single weekly check pays out at most 40% of the pool. No member receives more than ₹1,000 in a week. The amount is shared between active members in proportion to the days they were active. The pool can never go below zero.

## Small societies
A society needs at least 5 members to be checked on its own. Smaller ones are covered by the district check.

## Status of these numbers
The trigger level, the 40% cap, the ₹1,000 cap, the 8 days in 28 and the 5-member minimum are the starting values. [[TO BE CONFIRMED: the federations still need to confirm or change them.]]
