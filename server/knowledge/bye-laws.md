# Society bye-laws and how societies work

## The structure
Workers organise into cooperative societies. Societies are affiliated to a district federation, and district federations sit under a state federation. A society has a leader and members. Members hold shares in the society.

## Surplus
What a society earns from the 5% society share of the service fee is surplus. It is distributed to members in proportion to their shares, after the society's costs.

## Rate floors
A society leader can set a minimum rate for each service, and members cannot publish a rate below it. No rate can be below the statutory minimum wage for the work.
