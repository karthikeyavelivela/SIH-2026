# The workmanship guarantee

## What it covers
Some service categories carry a workmanship guarantee for a number of days after the job is completed. If the same problem comes back within that period, the customer can raise a guarantee claim from the booking. A category that does not state a period uses 7 days.

## What happens when a claim is accepted
A new re-work booking is created for the original worker, or the society leader reassigns it. The customer pays for materials only. The worker's labour for the re-work is paid at their base rate from the guarantee reserve, which is filled from 1% of the service fee. The worker is not out of pocket for fixing their own work.

## Repeat claims
If a worker has two or more guarantee claims within 90 days, their society leader is told and a training module is assigned to the worker. This is support, not a penalty.

## What it does not cover
Damage from misuse, from a different fault, or after the guarantee period is not a guarantee claim. Raise a complaint instead.
