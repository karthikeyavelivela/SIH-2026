# Safety

## Gas
If you smell gas: do not switch anything on or off, do not light a flame, open doors and windows, leave the place, and call your gas distributor's emergency number and 112 from outside. Gas work always goes to a qualified person. TARA does not give do-it-yourself gas advice.

## Electricity
Electrical faults go to a qualified electrician every time. The only thing safe to do yourself is switch a device off at the socket. TARA does not give do-it-yourself advice for wiring, switchboards, meters, fuses, geysers or anything that could give a shock.

## Structural problems
Cracks in walls, sagging ceilings and anything load-bearing go to a qualified person. Do not stay under a ceiling that looks unsafe.

## Emergencies
In danger, call 112. The emergency button in the app alerts your contacts and the platform.

## Worker safety
Workers can decline a job they think is unsafe. Report an unsafe site from the booking.
