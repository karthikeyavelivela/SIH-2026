# Booking, completion and cancellation

## How a booking runs
The customer books. Nearby workers or societies get an offer and one accepts. When the job starts, the customer is shown a 4-digit completion code. The worker finishes with a proof photo and that code, and the job is complete.

## If the code is not given
If the worker finishes without the code, the job waits for the customer to confirm. The customer can confirm it is done, or report a problem. A reported problem opens a dispute and holds the payment.

## Automatic confirmation
If the customer does nothing, a finished job is confirmed automatically after 24 hours, unless a problem has been reported.

## Urgent bookings
An urgent booking goes to the front of the queue and looks for workers in widening rings, about 3 km, then 6 km, then 10 km. There is no extra fee for urgent.

## Rating before the next booking
After a completed job both sides rate each other. The next booking is held until that rating is done.
