# Frequently asked questions

## Does a worker pay to join or to take jobs?
No. A worker receives 100% of their own rate. The 10% service fee is paid by the customer on top.

## How is identity checked?
Workers upload identity documents. They are kept in private storage and opened only through short-lived links for the owner, admins and managers who review them. Please upload only a masked Aadhaar.

## Can I use the app in Telugu or Hindi?
Yes. Change the language from the profile screen or the language selector on the sign-in page.

## I forgot my password
There is no self-service reset yet, because it needs a verified channel such as SMS. Ask your society leader or contact support, who can help you get back in.

## A worker has no phone
A society leader can add a member who has no phone and manage their papers, availability and payout details with their consent. The member can claim the account later with a code from the leader and their own phone number.

## How do I see or delete my data?
From your profile you can see your consent, withdraw the optional analytics consent, download a copy of your data, and delete your account. Deletion is blocked only while a booking or payout is still open.

## Who do I talk to about a problem?
First use the booking or complaint screens. TARA can point you there and offer to pass your question to a person.
