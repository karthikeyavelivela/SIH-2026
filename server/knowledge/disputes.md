# Disputes and complaints

## Who looks at a dispute first
A dispute goes first to the worker's society leader. For a solo worker with no society it goes to the district federation.

## How it moves up
If it is not resolved, it moves up one level: society, then district federation, then state federation, then the platform admins. Each level has 48 hours. If nothing happens in that time it moves up by itself. Admins can act at any level.

## While a dispute is open
The payment for that job is held. It is released or adjusted when the dispute is resolved.

## Complaints
A complaint is for something that is not a dispute about one specific job, for example behaviour or an app problem. Raise it from the complaints screen.

## Talking to a person
TARA cannot resolve a dispute, change a fare or issue a refund. It can point to the right screen and offer to pass the question to a person.
